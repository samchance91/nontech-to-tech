register 1 · 0–100ms

Instant

Press, hover, focus. Purely visual, no logic. Tab through these — every control shows a yellow focus ring; press shows a state.

register 2 · 100–400ms

Confirmation

The action already happened (optimistic). It's reversible — a toast confirms and offers undo, and pauses its own timer while you hover or focus it.

register 3

Evaluative

The answer response — never a bare "Wrong." Always what happened, why, and one next step. A correct answer gets an equally specific response.

register 4

Progressive

Journey-level. Progress updates at the moment of the action, not on next load — the node fills, the avatar walks, the instrument line moves. This is a preview of the signature element, the Atlas.
