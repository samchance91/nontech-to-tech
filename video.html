The VideoBlock

Every video is a facade — a self-drawn poster in a reserved 16:9 frame. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play; then it embeds through youtube‑nocookie.com, never an outbound link. Chapters seek in place, captions default on, speed is adjustable, and watching feeds the same adaptive signals as everything else. If a video can't load, you get the idea in text — never a dead frame.
