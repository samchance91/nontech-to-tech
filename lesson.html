One tagged tree, four tiers

Every concept is authored once as a list of tagged blocks — no duplicated courses. The tier reshapes the same tree: it reorders the blocks, decides how much scaffolding to show, paces them one-at-a-time or all-at-once, and expands or tucks away the enrichment. Switch tiers and watch the same concept change.
